TIMELINE OF AMERICAN COLONIZATION IN THE PHILIPPINES

1898TREATY

Treaty of Paris & Start of American Rule

Spain cedes the Philippines to the United States for $20M under the Treaty of Paris following the Battle of Manila Bay. This marks the beginning of official American rule and the controversial 'benevolent assimilation' policy.

1899WAR

Outbreak of the Philippine-American War

Armed conflict erupts between United States forces and the First Philippine Republic led by Emilio Aguinaldo in San Juan, Manila. The conflict arises from the rejection of Filipino sovereignty by U.S. forces.

1901GOVERNMENT

Capture of Aguinaldo & Civil Rule Setup

General Emilio Aguinaldo is captured in Palanan, Isabela. Shortly after, the United States establishes a civilian government, with William Howard Taft sworn in as the first civil governor of the islands.

1902LEGISLATION

The Philippine Organic Act (Philippine Bill)

U.S. Congress passes the Philippine Bill of 1902, which provides for a bicameral setup and authorizes the creation of a fully elected lower house assembly once peace is established across the country.

1907ASSEMBLY

Inauguration of the Philippine Assembly

The first national legislative assembly elected completely by Filipinos is formally convened, marking the first major structural step toward home-rule and legislative self-governance.

1916AUTONOMY

The Jones Law & All-Filipino Legislature

The Philippine Autonomy Act replaces the Organic Act, explicitly promising eventual national independence as soon as a stable government can be verified, and replacing the commission layout with a full democratic senate.

1934TRANSITION

The Tydings-McDuffie Independence Act

U.S. Congress establishes a definitive 10-year transition roadmap toward full sovereign nationhood. The framework authorizes the framing of a national constitution and a transitional regional commonwealth government.

1935COMMONWEALTH

Inauguration of the Commonwealth Government

The Commonwealth of the Philippines is officially proclaimed. Manuel L. Quezon is inaugurated as national President, signaling autonomous internal administration under structural American protection.

1941OCCUPATION

WWII & Japanese Military Occupation

Imperial Japanese forces invade the archipelago immediately following Pearl Harbor. The Commonwealth government goes into foreign exile while local underground guerilla and joint military defense campaigns carry out extensive resistance.

1945LIBERATION

Allied Forces Return & National Liberation

Combined American and Filipino military forces complete a massive counter-offensive to liberate the islands from axis occupation, restoring the civil authority of the Philippine Commonwealth in Manila.

1946SOVEREIGNTY

Declaration of Philippine Independence

The United States withdraws all colonial sovereignty over the islands. The Republic of the Philippines is inaugurated as a fully independent, sovereign country, fulfilling the timeline set by the Tydings-McDuffie roadmap.
